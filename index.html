Heritage Insurance — AML/KYC Platform Decision

Navigating a strategic platform architecture decision under information asymmetry and Phase 1 delivery guardrails

CPO
Escalation owned

Challenge: Heritage Insurance (currently on TurnQuest V4, ePay module only) initiated a request via their ICT lead to build AML/KYC functionality. The request, if fulfilled on V4, would violate the programme's Phase 1 guardrail — no new V4 PL/SQL dependencies — and pre-empt the v2.0 upgrade path. Heritage was unaware of the v2.0 programme, creating information asymmetry.

My role: Identified the strategic conflict, preserved information asymmetry appropriately, structured the decision options (build on V4 vs. defer to v2.0 upgrade vs. third-party KYC API), and escalated to CPO and CGIO with a documented recommendation before any client commitment was made.

Outcome: Escalation framework preserved programme integrity, prevented a technical debt commitment, and set the foundation for a structured Heritage v2.0 upgrade conversation. Smile ID recommended as KYC provider for Phase 1 multi-market coverage.
